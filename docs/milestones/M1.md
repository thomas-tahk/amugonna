# Milestone 1: Cook from my pantry — PLAN

_Plan written 2026-10-28 by chief (unattended cloud run). The owner pre-authorised this build in the scheduled prompt of 2026-10-28; the report replaces this page at milestone end._

**Done-gate:** "On my phone or my computer I put what's in my fridge and pantry into Amugonna, and within seconds of finishing I see recipes I can make with it, open one, and cook the meal from that screen."

Exercised as: a Playwright run at 390x844 (phone) and 1280x800 (desktop) that signs up, ticks common items + types free-text items, sees a non-empty "Cook now" list, opens a recipe and sees ingredients (have / missing) and numbered steps. Results must render in under 1 second after the pantry is saved.

## Recipe source, in one line
Bulk-scraping recipe sites into a public app is legally risky, so the base is the free, licence-clean TheMealDB catalogue (imported with attribution into our own Postgres by a script, not committed to git); social-media / URL recipes come later as a user's own "bring your own" import that is never redistributed.

## Cut from M1 (and why)
- **Wikibooks Cookbook import** — wikitext is free-form and needs a real parser plus hand review; the cloud sandbox can't reach wikibooks.org to build it against real pages. Moved to M2 if the TheMealDB match report shows the base is thin.
- **Dietary / allergy filtering** — tables exist but filtering needs the normalized vocabulary first; M2.
- **Photo-to-pantry, AI re-rank, BYO import, deploy** — later milestones per the owner's answers.
- **Grocery links** — only a "copy missing items" button (nearly free).

## Hosting options (owner picks; none idle-pause)
Prices are from third-party 2026 pricing summaries, not the providers' own pages; confirm before buying.
1. **Railway Hobby** — $5/month minimum that includes $5 of usage; app + Postgres metered on top, realistically ~$5-20/month for this app.
2. **DigitalOcean** (the original CLAUDE.md choice) — a Basic droplet (~$6/month) running the app and Postgres yourself, or App Platform (~$5/month) + Managed Postgres (~$15/month) for no server upkeep.
3. **Render paid** — Starter web service (~$7/month, always on) + paid Postgres (~$6/month+); the free tier sleeps and its free Postgres expires, so not that.
